Human decision and rollback

Your goalDefine the scope of human approval, prepare a rollback to a specific version and distinguish the request from its verified effect.

✿01 See the idea

In a fictional bicycle repair workshop, rule A ranks work tickets. A candidate rule B has just been compared with A. The technical dossier seems strong enough to propose a limited trial. The manager can still refuse it: she must judge what will change, the possible consequences and how to return to A.

Imagine a railway switch. Choosing B changes the route for the next tickets; keeping A allows you to prepare the return route. But a ticket already processed does not return to its initial state on its own. Returning to a previous rule and undoing all its effects are different operations.

Here, human approval covers a specific version of B, a designated queue and a trial lasting at most 10 min. The rollback is prepared for a specific version of A. Another version, another queue or an extension requires a decision suited to that new scope.

The entire scene, decisions and durations on this page are fictional. We calculate no improvement in ranking. We study the transition from an acceptable dossier to an authorized action, then the evidence of a rollback. The system’s actual mechanisms will be read separately in the milestone.

The rule to remember

A technical assessment prepares the decision; approval defines the scope of the action; a rollback is prepared before the trial and demonstrated after restoration.

✿02 Follow an example

Worked example

Change the rule while keeping a rollback path

In the fictional scene, A is the retained reference and B is the frozen candidate. The technical dossier allows a trial to be proposed; a rollback rehearsal on a copy of the queue has been verified. The human decision is initially missing. Let us prepare it, then calculate the rollback time described by this rehearsal.

  1. Define exactly what can change

    The dossier identifies A and B by their content fingerprints, meaning signatures calculated from the bytes of each version. It also retains the ranking configuration and the dependencies needed by A. The name “B” alone is insufficient: changed bytes produce a different object to examine.

    In this example, only the choice of rule changes. The ticket format remains compatible with A and no irreversible transformation of the queue is authorized. The dossier contains the comparison result, its limitations, the target queue, the manager responsible for the decision and the person who will verify the rollback. We do not repeat the comparison from the previous units here.

  2. Keep the conditions separate rather than averaging their assessments

    T means “technical dossier acceptable for this trial”; H means “explicit human approval applicable to this candidate, queue and duration”; R means “rollback prepared, compatible and tested on a copy within the stated budget”. These indicators are dimensionless. We encode an established condition as 1; a condition that is refused, missing or unverified as 0. The latter code does not explain the cause of the block: the dossier must specify it.

    O=T×H×R

    O is the dimensionless indicator that the conditions for starting are all met. The product equals 1 only if all the conditions equal 1; a single 0 factor makes it 0. This encoding represents a logical conjunction: an excellent technical dossier cannot compensate for missing approval. It measures neither a probability of safety nor overall quality.

  3. Calculate what remains blocked without approval

    The technical dossier is acceptable: T = 1. The rollback has been prepared: R = 1. No applicable human decision has yet been recorded: H = 0. A must remain in use; silence does not become approval.

    O=1×0×1=0×1=0

    Check by another route: ask “does approval for this specific scope exist?”. A negative answer is enough to block the trial, without multiplication. The formula must give the same decision.

  4. Make the human decision concrete and limited

    The manager receives the complete dossier and can accept, refuse or defer. In the accepted branch of our account, she authorizes use of the identified B on this queue alone for at most 10 min, under monitoring. She defines any urgent ticket placed behind an ordinary ticket as a stopping trigger. The trial also stops when its time expires; continuing requires a new decision.

    The instruction to roll back to the retained A is approved separately in the same dossier, for this trigger and target. The person responsible for executing it and the response to failure are designated before the trial. If the approval channel does not allow this advance commitment, the rollback must remain pending explicit approval; it cannot be claimed to be immediately executable.

    In our branch where this applicable approval exists, H = 1. The calculation becomes 1 × 1 = 1, then 1 × 1 = 1. The conditions are met for this bounded trial. This demonstrates neither that it has started nor that it has succeeded. Any modification of B requires T and H to be reexamined.

  5. Calculate the full rollback time

    The fictional rehearsal timed successive phases without overlap, starting when the alert was received: d with subscript “gel” is 18 s to stop new assignments and stabilize those in progress; d with subscript “rest” is 27 s to restore the selection of A and its configuration; d with subscript “contr” is 15 s to verify the result. Each d is a duration in seconds.

    D=dgel+drest+dcontr

    D is the total time until verified rollback, in seconds. First add 18 + 27 = 45 s, because stopping assignments precedes restoration. Then add verification: 45 + 15 = 60 s. Stopping at 45 s would omit the evidence of the result.

    The rollback budget is set before the trial at 75 s. The margin M is the budget minus the time D: 75 − 60 = 15 s. M is a signed duration in seconds; a negative margin indicates an overrun. Reverse check: 60 + 15 = 75 s. The time in this rehearsal is therefore below the budget.

    This margin is an arithmetic reserve for the rehearsed case, not a guaranteed maximum duration in the workshop. It does not cover an unknown detection delay before the alert. Real use would also require justification of the budget, the failure cases and how representative the rehearsal is.

  6. Stop, restore and retain the record

    Now suppose the trigger is observed during the fictional trial. The operator stops new assignments by B and handles work in progress according to the prepared instruction. The operator keeps pending tickets and previously recorded events, then restores the identified A and its configuration through the specifically authorized action.

    The log distinguishes the rollback request, the applicable approval, the attempt, its result and verification. It does not replace failure with an account of success. If restoration fails or the target is unknown, the queue remains in the planned stopped state and the manager is alerted; B is not restarted to make the alarm disappear.

  7. Check what the component reading the rule actually uses

    The presence of A on disk and the word “completed” do not prove the switch. Read back the fingerprint of the rule actually loaded by the component that ranks the next tickets, then compare it with the expected A. This readback must follow the current attempt: a past success does not prove it.

    Next, replay a prepared check case in an isolated copy: an urgent ticket and an ordinary ticket must return to the reference order, urgent first. This small case helps check the path in use; alone, it does not demonstrate complete version identity. It complements the fingerprint readback. Also check that pending tickets are retained before any authorized resumption.

    Only after these checks does the stopwatch in our rehearsal stop at 60 s. The finding must name the version, the attempt and the observations. If any is missing, write “rollback not established” and keep resumption pending.

  8. Check the limits of the word rollback

    If A has not been retained, if the tickets are no longer compatible or if restoration has not been tested, R remains 0. Even with a favorable dossier and approval, O = 1 × 0 = 0: the trial must not start under the stated conditions.

    Restoring A governs future assignments. It does not undo a repair already started because of B or erase the records of the incident. These effects require a separate examination. Finally, A itself may have become unsuitable: a stopped state or another safe solution must then be chosen, without assuming that “previous” always means “acceptable”.

Explain it in your own words

Hide the calculations. Explain why the dossier does not provide approval, why verification is included in the rollback time and what would need to be observed to state that A is actually in use. Finish with an effect that this rollback cannot undo.

✿03 Common mistakes

  • “The technical dossier is favorable, so B can be activated.”

    Look for a decision applicable to the bytes of B, the queue and the duration. Without it, H = 0 and O = 0. The remedy is to submit this dossier to the authorized person, who may still refuse or defer.

    Revisit the calculation without approval and the decision for the specific scope

  • “A is backed up: the rollback must be ready.”

    The copy must correspond to an identifiable version, remain compatible with the tickets and be restorable through a tested procedure. Also check approval for the action and the time through verification. The presence of a copy does not provide all this evidence.

    Review the rollback path and its phases

  • “The rollback replied “completed”, so everything is back as it was.”

    After this attempt, read back the rule actually loaded, replay the check case and check pending tickets. An effect already produced may persist. A tool reply attests only what it covers; alone, it does not demonstrate complete rollback.

Exercise 1 · Application · justified decision

Approval covers yesterday’s version

The manager had accepted the frozen B. A modification has just changed its bytes; the queue and duration remain identical. To isolate the approval question, you are given T = 1 and R = 1 for this modification. What H can you establish? Calculate O and write the next permitted action.

Hint 1

The displayed name is still B. Compare this name with the specific object that had been accepted.

Hint 2

A decision on previous content does not prove a decision on the modified content.

Compare with the solution

The approval received does not cover the new bytes. No applicable approval is established: H = 0. Therefore O = 1 × 0 × 1 = 0. The other conditions do not compensate for this gap.

Keep A in use and submit the modified candidate with its new fingerprint and the corresponding dossier. The manager can accept, refuse or defer. In a real dossier, T would also need to be reexamined after modification; it was fixed as favorable only by assumption in this exercise.

Check your reasoning

  • You link H to the specific version, rather than the name B alone.
  • You obtain O = 0 and propose a new decision before any start.
  • You distinguish the given assumption about T from what an actual modification would require.

Exercise 2 · Calculation · checking a budget

Verification takes longer

Before the workshop trial, a new fictional rehearsal keeps 18 s for stopping and 27 s for restoration, but verification takes 35 s. The budget remains 75 s. Calculate the full time, the margin and any overrun. Can the trial start solely on the basis of the approval already received?

Rate your confidence before comparing

Assisted answer: try this exercise without help later.

Hint 1

Rollback is complete only after verification. Add all the successive phases.

Hint 2

A negative margin means that the time in this rehearsal exceeds the stated budget.

Compare with the solution

Stopping and restoration still give 18 + 27 = 45 s. With the new verification, D = 45 + 35 = 80 s. The margin is 75 − 80 = -5 s. The overrun is therefore 80 − 75 = 5 s.

Independent check of the sign: 75 + 5 = 80 s, and the total exceeds the budget. The rollback dossier can no longer be considered acceptable under the stated condition: R = 0 and O = 0 despite the earlier approval.

Keep A, revise the procedure or propose another justified budget, then have the relevant decision reexamined. Silently changing the budget after seeing this result would remove the criterion instead of meeting it. These fictional rehearsals provide no bound on actual duration.

Check your reasoning

  • You include verification in 80 s and express the margin -5 s with its sign.
  • You recover the overrun of 5 s through the reverse check.
  • You suspend the start and make visible the condition that is no longer acceptable.

Exercise 3 · Transfer · challenging insufficient evidence

The reported restoration differs from the loaded rule

During the same fictional trial, the alert stopped assignments. Rollback to A was requested with the required approval and the tool displays “completed”. Yet the current readback indicates that the ranking component still has B loaded. A ticket has already been sent for repair. Write the finding to retain, the approach to resumption and the evidence still needed.

Assisted answer: try this exercise without help later.

Hint 1

The presence of A in the backup folder does not tell you which rule the component uses.

Hint 2

Separate restoration for future tickets from handling the ticket already in progress.

Compare with the solution

The finding must preserve the discrepancy: the rollback attempt was reported as completed, but B is still loaded in the current readback; actual rollback to A is not established. Keep new assignments stopped and report this contradiction to the designated manager.

Determine which consumer has not taken the change into account, then prepare the authorized corrective action. After this specific attempt, read back the fingerprint actually loaded, check that it matches A, replay the isolated check case and check pending tickets. Resumption remains subject to the prepared instruction and this evidence.

The ticket already sent still requires separate examination: replacing a rule in memory does not physically return the repair to its starting point. Retain the incident record and the actual elapsed time; neither the earlier success of a rehearsal nor the tool message should erase the current failure.

Check your reasoning

  • You state the observed discrepancy and keep resumption pending.
  • You request a readback after the attempt, from the component that uses the rule.
  • You combine version identity, a check case and ticket retention without claiming that a single test proves everything.
  • You explicitly distinguish the effect already produced from the rule for future assignments.

✿05 Recall from memory

Close the lesson, answer each question, then reveal the answer. Explain any missing steps in your own words.

Recall 1What must approval for the trial of B cover?

The candidate identified by its bytes, the action, the target queue, the maximum duration and the stopping conditions. A decision on another version or scope is insufficient. The rollback action must also have applicable approval.

Recall 2Why multiply T, H and R instead of averaging them?

Each indicator encodes a necessary condition: 1 if it is established, 0 otherwise. The product is 0 as soon as a condition is missing; a favorable technical dossier cannot compensate for missing approval or an unprepared rollback. This product proves no execution.

Recall 3When does the rollback time calculated in the example start and end?

It starts when the alert is received and ends after the checks: 18 + 27 + 15 = 60 s. The detection delay before the alert is outside this calculation. The margin of 15 s in this fictional rehearsal does not guarantee the duration of an actual rollback.

Recall 4What should be checked after a “rollback completed” message?

After the current attempt, read back the fingerprint of the version actually loaded by the component using it, compare it with A, replay the check case and check pending tickets. The presence of a copy or an old record of success does not prove the current switch.

Recall 5Why does returning to A not undo all the effects of B?

Rollback changes the rule for the next assignments. A ticket already processed or a repair already started retains its effects; handling them requires a separate decision. A must also remain acceptable and compatible with the present state.

Reconstruct the reasoning

Without rereading, reconstruct the sequence: identify A and B and the limits of the dossier; obtain a decision on this specific action; prepare and test the rollback; bound the trial; stop when the trigger occurs; restore through the authorized action; verify the version actually in use and the tickets; handle irreversible effects separately. Explain why no step can be replaced by the simple word “completed”.
